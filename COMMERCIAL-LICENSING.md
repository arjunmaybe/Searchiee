## Requesting commercial permission

A commercial licence may permit an organisation or individual to:

- Use Searchiee in business operations.
- Sell or commercially distribute original or modified versions.
- Incorporate Searchiee into commercial products.
- Operate commercial hosted services based on Searchiee.
- Keep independently authored modifications private.

Commercial rights are granted only through a separate written agreement. No commercial permission is granted merely by downloading the source code or application.

## Proposed commercial terms

The project's current proposed starting terms are:

- **Royalty:** 5% of attributable net receipts from the covered Searchiee-based product or service.
- **Annual minimum guarantee:** required, with the amount and currency negotiated separately.
- **Minimum guarantee treatment:** credited against royalties for the relevant contract year, rather than charged on top of the full royalty.
- **Private modifications:** permitted under the commercial agreement.

The proposed royalty calculation is based on receipts attributable to the covered product or service, not the licensee's total company revenue. The parties would need to agree in writing on permitted deductions, bundled-product revenue allocation, reporting, payment dates, record retention, audit rights, taxes, term, termination and other commercial details.

**These are proposed negotiating terms, not a binding public offer or an automatic royalty obligation under the PolyForm community licence.** The applicable commercial rights and payment obligations exist only to the extent expressly agreed in a separate written commercial agreement.

## Commercial enquiries

For a commercial licensing enquiry, open an issue in the [Searchiee repository](https://github.com/arjunmaybe/Searchiee/issues) with the title "Commercial licensing enquiry" and request a private contact channel.

Do not post confidential business, financial or personal information in a public issue.
